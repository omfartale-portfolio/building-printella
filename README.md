
# PRINTELLA
### Building a Consumer Brand from Zero
<img align="centre" width="280" src="IMG_2039.jpg" alt="Printella candle display">


Printella is a D2C scented candle and lifestyle brand I founded and developed from the ground up.

This repository documents the journey of building Printella — from the initial idea and product development to branding, operations, marketing, partnerships and sales.

Rather than presenting only the final outcome, I want to document the decisions, experiments, challenges and lessons behind building a consumer business.

## The Journey

I started Printella with the goal of creating an accessible yet premium-feeling scented candle brand.

I was involved across the business, taking ownership of:

- **Brand development:** Brand identity, positioning, packaging and customer experience.
- **Product development:** Candle development, fragrance selection, product testing and pricing.
- **Operations:** Raw material sourcing, inventory, production and fulfilment.
- **Marketing:** Instagram strategy, content creation, campaigns and digital marketing.
- **Business development:** Corporate partnerships, sponsorships and customer acquisition.
- **Sales:** Managing the sales process and growing the customer base.

## Key Milestones

- Founded and developed a D2C consumer brand.
- Sold 1,000+ products.
- <img align="right" width="280" src="IMG_2171.jpg" alt="Printella candle display">
- Secured corporate sponsorships from Starbucks and Domino's for college events.
- Built and managed the brand's online presence.
- Managed product development, sourcing, operations and marketing.

## What I Learned

Building Printella gave me practical experience in:

- Taking a business idea from concept to execution.
- Understanding consumer behaviour and product positioning.
- Managing costs, pricing and supply chains.
- Building a brand through content and digital channels.
- Developing partnerships and negotiating commercial opportunities.
- Making decisions with limited resources.
- Learning from experiments that did not work as expected.

## What This Repository Contains

This repository will progressively document:

1. The original idea and business model.
2. Brand positioning and product strategy.
3. Product development and supply chain.
4. Pricing and unit economics.
5. Marketing and customer acquisition.
6. Corporate partnerships and business development.
7. Challenges, experiments and lessons learned.
