
# 02 — Brand & Product Development

## Building the Product Range

One of my primary responsibilities at Printella was developing the product range from scratch.

I experimented with different candle formats, designs and product concepts to create a diverse collection rather than limiting the brand to conventional jar candles.

### Product Categories

The product range included:

- **Jar Candles:** Traditional container-based scented candles.
- **Mould Candles:** Candles created using different moulds and shapes.
- **Decorative Candles:** Design-focused candles intended to add aesthetic value.
- **Secret Message Candles:** Candles incorporating a hidden-message concept.
- **Dessert Candles:** Dessert-inspired decorative candle designs.
- **Diya Candles:** Candle products inspired by traditional Indian diyas.

## Fragrance Development

An important part of product development was creating distinctive fragrance combinations.

Rather than relying entirely on ready-made fragrance blends, I experimented with individual fragrance oils and developed my own combinations through mixing and matching different oils.

This involved exploring different fragrance profiles and identifying combinations that could become part of Printella's product range.

## Experimentation and Quality Testing

I personally experimented with:

- Different wax mixtures.
- Fragrance oil proportions.
- Fragrance combinations.
- Burning performance and burn time.
- Overall product quality and consistency.

These experiments helped me understand the practical relationship between raw materials, formulation, product performance and the final customer experience.

## End-to-End Manufacturing

Printella followed a hands-on manufacturing model.

I personally manufactured the candles rather than outsourcing production.

This gave me direct involvement in the complete product development process, from experimenting with materials and formulations to making the finished products.

## Key Skills Developed

- Product development and experimentation.
- Fragrance formulation and blending.
- Hands-on manufacturing.
- Product testing and quality control.
- Creative problem-solving.
- Understanding raw materials and product performance.

# 02 — Brand, Positioning & Product Development

## 1. The Origin Story

Printella was initially conceived as a clothing brand.

I had already secured the brand name and domain with the intention of entering the fashion industry. However, after evaluating the capital required to establish a clothing business, I realised that it would require a larger initial investment than I wanted to make.

I wanted to build a business independently using my personal savings rather than depending on external funding.

Instead of abandoning the idea of entrepreneurship, I adapted the business model and decided to launch Printella as a scented candle brand using the same brand identity.

This decision allowed me to start with a more manageable investment while retaining ownership of the venture.

What began as an alternative business idea eventually developed into a D2C consumer brand.

## 2. Brand Positioning & Target Customers

Printella was developed as a scented candle and gifting brand, with an emphasis on fragrance, aesthetics, product presentation and customer experience.

The intended customer segments included:

- Women interested in home fragrance and lifestyle products.
- Teenagers and young adults.
- Customers purchasing gifts for special occasions.
- Customers looking for aesthetically appealing home décor.
- Buyers interested in personalised and creative gifting options.

## 3. Brand Identity & Creative Development

I independently worked on developing Printella's visual identity and customer-facing brand experience.

My responsibilities included:

- Developing the brand's visual direction.
- Creating packaging concepts and product labels.
- Designing gift boxes and gifting experiences.
- Exploring product presentation and aesthetics.
- Using AI tools to support creative ideation and design development.

I used AI as a creative and execution aid while retaining ownership of the brand's creative decisions.

## 4. Product Development

I developed a diverse product range rather than limiting the business to conventional jar candles.

The range included:

- Jar candles.
- Mould candles.
- Decorative candles.
- Secret message candles.
- Dessert-inspired candles.
- Diya candles.

## 5. Fragrance Formulation

I experimented with individual fragrance oils and developed my own fragrance combinations by mixing different available oils.

This involved exploring fragrance profiles and experimenting with combinations to create distinctive product experiences.

## 6. Experimentation & Quality Testing

I personally experimented with:

- Different wax mixtures.
- Fragrance oil proportions.
- Fragrance combinations.
- Burning performance and burn time.
- Product quality and consistency.

This provided practical experience in understanding how raw materials and formulation decisions influence the final product.

## 7. End-to-End Manufacturing

Printella followed a hands-on manufacturing model.

I personally manufactured the products rather than outsourcing production.

My involvement extended from raw material experimentation and fragrance formulation to manufacturing, packaging and preparation for fulfilment.

## 8. Pricing & Commercial Considerations

Pricing required consideration of the economics of individual products, including:

- Wax and fragrance oil costs.
- Packaging and labelling.
- Manufacturing requirements.
- Product positioning.
- Customer expectations and market alternatives.
- Selling and fulfilment expenses.

The objective was to balance customer affordability, perceived product value and business sustainability.
